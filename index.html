About these numbers

Netlas / ZoomEye columns
Per-engine measured populations from the curated cross-reference list. A dash means the product was not queried on that engine, or the result was too low to be meaningful — not zero.
Merged column
The census figure from the merged Netlas+ZoomEye product census, measured by product, title or app fingerprint. It is not the sum of the two engine columns: the engines crawl different corpora and overlap.
Rows with only engine columns
Present in the curated list but not carried into the merged census, usually because the population was too low to clear the census threshold or the match was noisy. The per-engine numbers still stand.
Noise flag
Two curated entries matched generic page titles across unrelated sites. Their counts are real matches for the string, not for the product.
Confidence
From the census: how the population was measured and how strongly it was tied to the product.

About this tracker

Checkboxes and notes are stored locally in this browser (localStorage) — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Download JSON to save a snapshot and Import to restore one or move it to another browser.

Every product row also has its own free-text note box. Notes are the working record: what you validated, which port, what the RCE path turned out to be, what you ruled out.

Aggregate exposure measurements only — product names, population counts, class/tier classification, RCE-path descriptions and public CVE IDs. No host addresses, no credentials, no customer data.
